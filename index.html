Built for one job: commissioning pixels

There's no show to program and no controller to configure. PixelCheck does the checks you'd otherwise do with a console and a lot of squinting.

Channel check

Step pixel by pixel or channel by channel with F and R. Light just red, green, blue or white to prove the colour order.

Console-style keypad

Type 1/1 thru 1/23 @ 75 the way you would on a lighting desk. Ranges can cross universes.

Test chases

White, R G B W and rainbow chases in patch order, so a gap or a jump shows exactly where the fault is.

Drag-and-drop patch

Pixel strips in any colour order and any length, plus white dimmers. Long strips roll into the next universe by themselves.

sACN and Art-Net

sACN multicast with a priority from 0 to 200, or Art-Net broadcast or unicast to a single node.

Looks after itself

Your patch is saved as you go, Help is one key away (F1), and new versions install themselves at launch.

Walk the run, pixel by pixel

  • By pixel lights one pixel at a time in patch order, with all channels or just one colour.
  • By channel lights one raw DMX channel, whether anything is patched there or not.
  • Jump straight to a pixel or an address, and set the test level.
  • The output view shows what every pixel is being sent right now.

Patch it the way it's wired

  • Pixel strips in RGBW, RGB, GRB or any other colour order, with any pixel count.
  • White dimmer channels for single-colour fixtures.
  • Drag a fixture onto the channel it starts at.
  • Overlaps and bad addresses are flagged as you patch.

Type levels like you're on a desk

Keypad levels sit on top of everything else until you release them. Addresses are universe/channel.

CommandDoes
1/1 thru 1/23 @ 75Universe 1, channels 1 to 23 at 75%
1/5 + 1/9 @ fullTwo channels at full
2/1 thru 2/512 - 2/100A whole universe except one channel
1/511 thru 2/4 @ outRanges can cross universes
@ 50The last selection at 50%

Blackout stops every chase and releases every held channel in one click.

Download

Install it once and it keeps itself up to date. A licence key, emailed when you buy, activates one computer and works offline for 30 days.

Mac

Apple silicon and Intel, in one download.

Drag it to Applications. If macOS won't open it, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. If it says the app is damaged, run this in Terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PixelCheck.app

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

If SmartScreen says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

Download for Windows

Your computer needs to be on the same network as your pixel controllers; for sACN multicast that usually means wired, on the lighting network. Moving to a new laptop? Use Help › Move to another computer on the old one, then enter your key on the new one. All files are on the release page.
